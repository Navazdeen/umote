umote case v7.2 - for the 6x6x9.5 tact switches. The shell is NOT changed: use your v7.1 shell.

Print only these:
  caps.stl                 caps, D-pad sectors, OK, both rockers (4.0 proud, deep hex bore, roofed pockets)
  and the board you use:
  button_board_print.stl   copper-tape board (raised traces). Copper side UP. Spacers: print
                           button_spacers.stl (5 posts) and glue them (CA) into the 5 shallow
                           round recesses on the board's bed side.
  button_board_holes.stl   holes-only board. Component side UP, posts printed on it.
  button_carrier.stl       module carrier. Component side UP, posts printed on it.

Everything else in this folder is byte-identical to v7.1.
The boards' long edges are chamfered 0.5 x 45 deg top and bottom: the bottom chamfer sits on the
v7.1 hooks' slope, and the 5 posts press the board onto it from the face (locked both ways).

Copper-tape board, v7.2: traces and pads grown into the free space (traces up to 2.0, rings round
the holes >= 0.5, switch rings ~0.9), >= 0.6 between nets. Holes are drill + 0.2 in the model.

Slicer (Orca / Bambu Studio names; PrusaSlicer is similar), 0.4 nozzle, PETG:
  Wall generator ............ Arachne
  Minimum feature size ...... 25 %        Minimum wall width ... 50 %
  Outer wall line width ..... 0.40        Walls ................ 2 (4 is fine for the 1.6 base)
  Layer height .............. 0.2 base; height range modifier from 1.6 mm up at 0.12 (the traces)
  Ironing ................... topmost surface only (flat trace tops for the copper tape)
  X-Y hole compensation ..... +0.1        Elephant foot compensation ... 0.15
  Solder .................... Sn42Bi58 low-temperature, iron ~200 C
