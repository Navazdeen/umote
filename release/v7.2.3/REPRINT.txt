umote case v7.2.3 - for the 6x6x9.5 tact switches.
v7.2.3: button_board_print.stl changed from v7.2.2 (and button_board_insulator.stl, whose ribs follow its
  traces). The MCP23017 (DIP-28) and Q1 holes are 1.0 x 1.6
  slots across the pin row (1.2 round before): the ring towards each neighbouring pin is 0.45 (0.35
  was dropped by the slicer), and the splayed legs get more room across the chip.
v7.2.2: button_board_print.stl and button_board_insulator.stl changed from v7.2.1.
  - The copper-tape board is 2.0 thick at every hole (2.6 before): 1.0 base inside a 1.6 rim, trace
    tops level at 2.0, so the legs come through 0.6 further. The MCP23017 (DIP-28) holes are 1.2 (0.9)
    and Q1's 1.1. Its switch side is unchanged, so the caps and spacers are the same.
  - The insulating tray is 1.4 narrower (it sits 0.6 higher now, clear of the clip hooks) and has
    1.0 to the battery.
v7.2.1: caps.stl - the switch pockets' corners no longer break through the cap walls.

Print only these:
  caps.stl                 caps, D-pad sectors, OK, both rockers (4.0 proud, deep hex bore, roofed pockets)
  and the board you use:
  button_board_print.stl   copper-tape board (raised traces). Copper side UP. Spacers: print
                           button_spacers.stl (5 posts) and glue them (CA) into the 5 shallow
                           round recesses on the board's bed side.
  button_board_insulator.stl  0.8 tray between the copper side and the battery (for a 4.2 battery;
                           1.0 clear): 0.4 floor, 1.5 rim, thin ribs that rest on trace tops between
                           the joints. Print it flat side down (a brim helps keep it flat). Lay it on
                           the traces after soldering, rim and ribs towards the board, and fix it
                           with dots of double-sided tape. Cut the legs flush and keep solder low.
                           Windows leave the 1206 parts and the J5 wires free.
  button_board_holes.stl   holes-only board. Component side UP, posts printed on it.
  button_carrier.stl       module carrier. Component side UP, posts printed on it.

Everything else in this folder is byte-identical to v7.1.
The boards' long edges are chamfered 0.5 x 45 deg top and bottom: the bottom chamfer sits on the
v7.1 hooks' slope, and the 5 posts press the board onto it from the face (locked both ways).

Copper-tape board, v7.2: traces and pads grown into the free space (traces up to 2.0, rings round
the holes >= 0.5, switch rings ~0.9), >= 0.6 between nets. Holes are drill + 0.2 in the model.
