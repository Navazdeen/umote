About sharp edges. An FDM printer draws outlines, so a crisp vertical corner prints as crisp as the nozzle allows (about 0.2 mm radius). What it can't do is hang material in the air, which the red faces would show. Creases here are where two flat faces meet, such as the 45° bevels and chamfers. They print fine; whether they feel too sharp in the hand is a design choice.
