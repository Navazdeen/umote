# Case v7.2: 9.5 mm tact switches, board clamped both ways

> **Revised 2026-11-24, after approval:** the user can't reprint the shell. The shell stays v7.1 (its hooks unchanged). Instead, the boards get 0.5 x 45 deg chamfers on their long edges, and the bottom ones sit on the hooks' slope. The spacers reach the face with a 0.05 preload (4.75 tall), and their spots moved to (17, 3.5), (6, 40.5), (35, 41.5), (11, 106) and (33.5, 103.5). The holes-only board gets them too, and now prints component side up. The "Board clips" item below is superseded.

Date: 2026-11-24. Base: v7.1 (`origin/master` 374e336, printed carrier for the CJMCU-2317 module).

## Why

The 6x6x7 tact switches can't be sourced. The replacements are 6x6x9.5:

- 9.5 mm in total: a 3.8 mm body under a 5.7 mm round plunger
- the plunger tapers from 3.5 mm at its base to 3.0 mm at its tip

With the board at its v7.1 height (top z 9.9), the plunger tips reach z 19.4, but the caps' tops are only at 19.6. So the board can't reach its clips. There are three more problems:

- **Clip hooks are upside down.** They face the board with their 45 deg slope and face the back with their flat side. The board meets a flat wall going in, and once it's in, nothing holds it down. The gap at the slope measures 43.0, the board's own width, so nothing grips it.
- **The rocker prints badly.** Its pockets for the switch bodies are 0.5 mm deep, with flat ceilings bridged in mid-air just above the bed, and they sagged on the print.
- **Nothing stops the board moving towards the face.**

## Goals

- The 9.5 mm switches fit, and every cap still clicks its switch.
- The board is held both ways: hooks stop it dropping back, spacers stop it moving towards the face.
- No bridges in any cap or rocker.
- Non-goals: changing the remote's thickness, the board outline (43.0 x 112, measured right), or the layout.

## Approaches considered

1. **Taller caps (picked by the user).** Only the caps, the shell (for the clip fix) and the carrier are reprinted. The caps stand 4.0 mm above the face instead of 3.0.
2. **A 2.5 mm thicker remote.** Caps keep their height, but every part is reprinted and the remote gets chunkier.
3. **Cut the plungers down 2.5 mm by hand.** No CAD changes, but 14 accurate cuts.

## Design

- **params**: `sw_body 3.8`, `sw_total 9.5`, `cap_proud 4.0`.
  - Derived: `act_top 19.4`, `cap_h 6.8`, `sock 5.6`, so 1.2 mm of cap stays above the plunger tip (the 0.4 mm marks stay engraved on top).
  - The rocker gets `rk_h 7.1` and `rk_sock 5.9`.
- **Bores**: the same hex bore (`grip_af 3.4`), made deeper (follows `sock`). It grips the plunger's 3.5 mm base, and the 3.0 mm tip sits loose inside it.
- **Switch-body pocket in every cap, OK, D-pad sector and rocker**:
  - why: the new body top (z 13.7) is 0.1 mm under the cap bottoms (13.8), and 0.2 mm *above* the rocker's bottom (13.5).
  - shape: a 6.6 mm square pocket centred on each switch, vertical for `pocket_h` (0.6 in caps, 1.0 in rockers to allow for the 0.3 tip and the travel). Then a 45 deg roof slopes in to the hex bore, so nothing is bridged.
  - The roof replaces the rocker's flat 0.5 mm pockets.
- **Board clips** (`shell.scad` `board_clip`):
  - the hook gets a flat ledge at z = `bb_z0` facing the face, and a 0.6 mm, 45 deg slope facing the back, so the board slides past it going in;
  - the clip gets 0.3 mm longer for the slope (its bottom ends 0.65 mm above the battery);
  - in print orientation the ledge is a 0.6 mm overhang.
- **Spacers on the carrier** (`pcb/make_carrier_stl.py`):
  - five posts, 4.0 mm across, rising from the carrier's top face to 0.1 mm under the face's underside (4.6 tall), so the board has 0.1 mm of play;
  - placed where the face underside is flat and clear of caps, the D-pad ring, the rockers, the clips, the wires, the mic module, Q1 and the module window. Board coordinates: (17.0, 3.5), (6.5, 39.0), (35.0, 41.5), (6.5, 100.5), (32.0, 102.0). The builder confirms each spot against the shell mesh and the caps pressed.
  - The carrier still prints top face up, and the posts print upright with no supports.
- **Checks**:
  - `fit_check_v7.py --module` with the new switch model and the spacers;
  - `case/tests/test_v7.py`, plus new tests: every spot meets the shell only at its top, no cap or rocker touches a switch body when pressed or tipped, the hook ledge sits at `bb_z0`, and no cap has a horizontal ceiling wider than the bore.
- **Release**: `release/v7.2/` holds the whole set (shell, caps, dpad ring, carrier and the unchanged parts), with SHA256SUMS. Uploading to copyparty only when asked.
